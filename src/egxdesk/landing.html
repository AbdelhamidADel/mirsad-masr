Where the data comes from

Plain sources already wired into MIRSAD MASR — no vanity logos, no invented partners.

  • Yahoo Finance — EGX quotes, FX, gold futures, Brent, and English wires when needed.
  • Egyptian Exchange (EGX) — official media-center announcements and disclosures.
  • Mubasher — company news and exchange announcements for listed names.
  • Starta Markets — Egypt gold and silver karat / fineness pages.
  • BBC & The Guardian — public world and business RSS for context headlines.
  • Google News — topic RSS (including Arabic EG feeds) when other wires are thin.
Quotes and metal prices are delayed and indicative. Jewelers add workmanship and margin. Nothing here is financial advice — always cross-check critical numbers with official sources.

FAQ

Short answers about access, delay, gold, and the API.

Is it free?

Yes. The dashboard and public API surface on a self-hosted instance are free to run. Some optional quote providers need your own API keys.

How delayed are the quotes?

Quotes are delayed — typical retail feed delay, not a paid real-time exchange feed. Treat numbers as indicative and refresh when you need a fresher read.

Where does the gold price come from?

Egypt karat and fineness prices are read from Starta Markets. Spot gold and related FX context also come through the markets feed (Yahoo) where available.

Is this financial advice?

No. MIRSAD MASR aggregates public market data for information only. It is not investment, legal, or tax advice.

Which exchange does it cover?

The Egyptian Exchange (EGX), plus USD/EGP, EUR/EGP, gold, silver, and Brent as supporting context.

Can I use the API?

Yes. Browse the interactive docs at /docs — endpoints under /v1/* power the same views you see in the dashboard.
